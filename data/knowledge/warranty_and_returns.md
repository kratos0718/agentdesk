# Warranty and Returns

Electronics come with a 1 year manufacturer warranty from the date of delivery. Warranty claims cover manufacturing defects, not physical or water damage.

Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery if they are unused and in their original packaging.

To start a return, the customer shares the order number and the reason for the return. A pickup is scheduled within 2 business days.

Replacement items are shipped once the returned item passes quality inspection, which usually takes 3 business days.
