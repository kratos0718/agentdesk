# Shipping Policy

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days within India. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and costs Rs. 149 extra.

Orders placed before 2 PM are dispatched the same day. Orders placed after 2 PM are dispatched the next business day.

Every shipped order has a tracking number. Customers can check the status of an order at any time by giving the order number to support.

If an order has been in transit for more than 7 business days, it is treated as delayed and the customer can ask for a free replacement or a full refund.

We currently ship only within India.
