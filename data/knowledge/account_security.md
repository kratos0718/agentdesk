# Account and Security Policy

Support staff will never ask a customer for their password, OTP or full card number.

To change the email address on an account, the customer must confirm the change from the old email address.

A customer can only view and manage orders that belong to their own account. Support cannot share details of another customer's order.

If a customer suspects that their account was accessed without permission, support should lock the account and ask the customer to reset their password.

Payment card numbers are never stored in plain text and must never appear in chat transcripts or logs.
