# Refund Policy

Customers can request a refund within 30 days of delivery. Orders that were never delivered can be refunded at any time.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method and usually reach the customer within 5 to 7 business days.

Refunds up to Rs. 5,000 are approved automatically by the support system. Refunds above Rs. 5,000 must be approved by a support supervisor before they are issued.

Digital products, gift cards and items marked "final sale" are not eligible for a refund.

A partial refund can be issued when only some items in an order are returned.
